00 — The shop

Browse, ask, and order — by hand or by agent

This is a real working bookshop page. The AgentifyJS library reads the live page and turns it into tool definitions an agent can call, and the browser runtime below runs a three-agent network (booknook_assistant) that answers questions, fills your cart, and checks out — with your confirmation before anything is ordered. Delivery is arranged with our partner site, Harbor Delivery Co., a second agent site that our network calls after you approve the contact. No server, no tracking, no payment: orders are demos and your LLM key (if you add one) never leaves your browser.

Conforms to the Minimum Viable Specification for a Worldwide Agentic Web (MVP 0.2): public agent-manifest.json, booknook.network.json, links.json and a browser runtime. See SPEC-CONFORMANCE.md for the mapping.
